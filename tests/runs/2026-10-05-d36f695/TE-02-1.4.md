Marketing Rule pre-review — SEC Rule 206(4)-1
Elements detected: testimonial
Reviewed as an advertisement under (e)(1); whether (e)(1) covers this communication is not assessed.
State-registered advisers are subject to their state's rule, which may differ from any flag below; differences are not resolved here.
Flags: 4. Confirm: 3.

Flag 1
Where: “"After two years with the firm, I finally understand my own plan."”
What: Embedded Google or Yelp-style reviews with no disclosure that the reviewers are current or former clients
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-03 · RA-2025-12
Fix: supply a disclosure stating whether the speaker is a current or former client

Flag 2
Where: “"After two years with the firm, I finally understand my own plan."”
What: Undated quotes attributed to "clients"
Cite: (b)(1)(i), (e)(17) · TE-13 · EA-2024-09
Fix: supply a disclosure stating whether the speaker is a current or former client

Flag 3
Where: "See disclosures."
What: "See disclosures" link; footnote-size text
Cite: (b)(1) · TE-02 · RA-2025-12
Fix: rewrite so the client-status, compensation, and conflict disclosures appear directly next to the testimonial in regular-size, prominent text instead of behind a link

Flag 4
Where: "in eight-point gray type at the bottom of the page"
What: Tiny/low-contrast/fast-scrolling disclosure text
Cite: (a)(7) · GP-12 · RA-2024-04
Fix: rewrite so the disclosure is presented in normal-sized, adequate-contrast text next to the testimonial instead of eight-point gray type at the page bottom
