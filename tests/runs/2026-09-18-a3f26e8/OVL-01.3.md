Flags: 5. Confirm: 1.

Flag 1
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Return figures in a post with no risk language
Cite: (a)(4) · PERF-08 · RA-2024-04
Fix: supply risk/limitation language (e.g., that past performance does not guarantee future results and investing involves risk of loss)

Flag 2
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Returns without dates; mixed periods
Cite: (a)(6) · PERF-10 · RA-2024-04
Fix: supply the specific start and end dates (or calendar year) for this performance period

Flag 3
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Gross figures with no net figure alongside
Cite: (d)(1) · PERF-16 · RA-2024-04
Fix: supply the net performance figure alongside, with equal prominence to the gross figure

Flag 4
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Quote with no status/compensation/conflict line
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-01 · RA-2025-12
Fix: supply a clear and prominent statement that this is a current client's testimonial, whether compensation was provided, and any material conflicts of interest

Flag 5
Where: "That's a note from a family I've worked with since 2022, shared with permission."
What: Undated quotes attributed to "clients"
Cite: (b)(1)(i); testimonial definition (e) · TE-13 · EA-2024-09
Fix: supply the date this testimonial was given
