Marketing Rule pre-review — SEC Rule 206(4)-1
Elements detected: testimonial
Reviewed as an advertisement under (e)(1); whether (e)(1) covers this communication is not assessed.
State-registered advisers are subject to their state's rule, which may differ from any flag below; differences are not resolved here.
Flags: 4. Confirm: 2.

Flag 1
Where: "Leave us a review on Google this month and we'll send you a $25 coffee gift card as a thank-you."
What: Any incentive for reviews
Cite: (b)(1), (b)(2)(i) · TE-04 · RA-2025-12
Fix: remove

Flag 2
Where: "Three years in and I've never felt more on top of my finances."
What: Quote with no status/compensation/conflict line
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-01 · RA-2025-12
Fix: supply a status/compensation/conflict disclosure accompanying the review wherever it is displayed, including on Google

Flag 3
Where: "Three years in and I've never felt more on top of my finances."
What: Embedded Google or Yelp-style reviews with no disclosure that the reviewers are current or former clients
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-03 · RA-2025-12
Fix: supply a disclosure on Google that the reviewer is a current or former client

Flag 4
Where: "there are no conflicts of interest arising from our relationship"
What: Any absolute no-conflict statement
Cite: (a)(1), (a)(2) · GP-01 · RA-2024-04
Fix: remove

Confirm 1
Where: "The reviewer is a current client who received a $25 gift card for the review"
Depends on: Does the firm have a written agreement with this reviewer covering the review activity and the gift-card compensation?
Cite: (b)(2)(ii)
Would apply: TE-08

Confirm 2
Where: "The reviewer is a current client who received a $25 gift card for the review"
Depends on: Was this reviewer checked for disqualifying events before being compensated for the review?
Cite: (b)(3)
Would apply: TE-10
