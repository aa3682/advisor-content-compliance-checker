Marketing Rule pre-review — SEC Rule 206(4)-1
Elements detected: testimonial
Reviewed as an advertisement under (e)(1); whether (e)(1) covers this communication is not assessed.
State-registered advisers are subject to their state's rule, which may differ from any flag below; differences are not resolved here.
Flags: 2. Confirm: 2.

Flag 1
Where: "Leave us a review on Google this month and we'll send you a $25 coffee gift card as a thank-you."
What: Any incentive for reviews
Cite: (b)(1), (b)(2)(i) · TE-04 · RA-2025-12
Fix: remove the $25 gift-card incentive for leaving a review

Flag 2
Where: "On Google, reviews appear exactly as written, without those details."
What: Embedded Google or Yelp-style reviews with no disclosure that the reviewers are current or former clients
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-03 · RA-2025-12
Fix: supply a clear and prominent disclosure, on the Google review itself, of the reviewer's current-client status, the compensation given, and the absence of conflicts

Confirm 1
Where: "received a $25 gift card for the review"
Depends on: Does the firm have a written agreement with the reviewer describing the scope of the review activity and the compensation terms?
Cite: (b)(2)(ii)
Would apply: TE-08

Confirm 2
Where: "received a $25 gift card for the review"
Depends on: Was the reviewer checked for disqualifying events or Commission actions before being compensated for the review?
Cite: (b)(3)
Would apply: TE-10
