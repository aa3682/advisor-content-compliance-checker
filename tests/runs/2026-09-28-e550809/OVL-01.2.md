Marketing Rule pre-review — SEC Rule 206(4)-1
Elements detected: testimonial, gross performance
Reviewed as an advertisement under (e)(1); whether (e)(1) covers this communication is not assessed.
State-registered advisers are subject to their state's rule, which may differ from any flag below; differences are not resolved here.
Flags: 6. Confirm: 5.

Flag 1
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Quote with no status/compensation/conflict line
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-01 · RA-2025-12
Fix: Supply the clear and prominent disclosures next to the quote: that it was given by a current client, whether any cash or non-cash compensation was provided, and a brief statement of any material conflicts of interest.

Flag 2
Where: "That's a note from a family I've worked with since 2022, shared with permission."
What: Undated quotes attributed to "clients"
Cite: (b)(1)(i), (e)(17) · TE-13 · EA-2024-09
Fix: Supply the date the note was given and state whether the family is a current or former client.

Flag 3
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them"
What: Gross figures with no net figure alongside
Cite: (d)(1) · PERF-16 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Supply net performance for the same period, presented with at least equal prominence and in a format that facilitates comparison, or remove the 14% figure.

Flag 4
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them"
What: Returns without fee/expense disclosure
Cite: (a)(1) · PERF-04 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Supply the fees and expenses that apply to the account, or remove the 14% figure.

Flag 5
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them"
What: Returns without dates; mixed periods
Cite: (a)(6) · PERF-10 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Replace "this year" with the exact start and end dates of the period, or remove the 14% figure.

Flag 6
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Return figures in a post with no risk language
Cite: (a)(4) · PERF-08 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Supply fair and balanced language on the material risks and limitations of the results, or remove the return figure and the "never worried" statement.
