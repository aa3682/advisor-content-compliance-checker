Marketing Rule pre-review — SEC Rule 206(4)-1
Elements detected: testimonial
Reviewed as an advertisement under (e)(1); whether (e)(1) covers this communication is not assessed.
State-registered advisers are subject to their state's rule, which may differ from any flag below; differences are not resolved here.
Flags: 3. Confirm: 1.

Flag 1
Where: "Those are app-store reviews of the software, and they say everything about the experience you can expect when we work together."
What: Third-party product testimonials or user reviews presented as evidence about the adviser
Cite: (a)(3) · GP-11 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Remove the software reviews, or delete the claim that they show the experience clients can expect from working with the firm.

Flag 2
Where: "Finally feel in control of my money."
What: Quote with no status/compensation/conflict line
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-01 · RA-2025-12
Fix: Remove the quotes, or supply beside them whether each speaker is a current client, whether compensation was provided, and any material conflict of interest.

Flag 3
Where: "Those are app-store reviews of the software"
What: Embedded Google or Yelp-style reviews with no disclosure that the reviewers are current or former clients
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-03 · RA-2025-12
Fix: Remove the reviews, or supply a disclosure stating whether the reviewers are current or former clients.

Confirm 1
Where: "PlanPath, the planning software behind every plan I build"
Depends on: Does the adviser receive compensation, cash or non-cash, from PlanPath or through a link or arrangement tied to this mention?
Cite: (b)(1)(i)(B)
Would apply: GP-06
