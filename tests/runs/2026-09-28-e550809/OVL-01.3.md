Marketing Rule pre-review — SEC Rule 206(4)-1
Elements detected: testimonial, gross performance
Reviewed as an advertisement under (e)(1); whether (e)(1) covers this communication is not assessed.
State-registered advisers are subject to their state's rule, which may differ from any flag below; differences are not resolved here.
Flags: 6. Confirm: 2.

Flag 1
Where: "My account is up 14% before fees this year with them, and I've never worried once."
What: Quote with no status/compensation/conflict line
Cite: (b)(1)(i) · TE-01 · RA-2025-12
Fix: Supply a clear and prominent line next to the quote stating whether the speaker is a current client, whether compensation was provided, and any material conflicts of interest.

Flag 2
Where: "up 14% before fees this year"
What: Returns without fee/expense disclosure
Cite: (a)(1) · PERF-04 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Supply the fees and expenses that were not deducted from the 14% figure, or remove the figure.

Flag 3
Where: "up 14% before fees this year"
What: Return figures in a post with no risk language
Cite: (a)(4) · PERF-08 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Supply fair and balanced risk language alongside the return figure, or remove the figure.

Flag 4
Where: "up 14% before fees this year"
What: Returns without dates; mixed periods
Cite: (a)(6) · PERF-10 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Replace "this year" with the exact start and end dates of the period measured.

Flag 5
Where: "up 14% before fees this year"
What: Gross figures with no net figure alongside
Cite: (d)(1) · PERF-16 · RA-2024-04
Fix: Supply net performance for the same period and methodology, with at least equal prominence, or remove the gross figure.

Flag 6
Where: "Grateful for clients like these."
What: Undated quotes attributed to "clients"
Cite: (b)(1)(i), (e)(17) · TE-13 · EA-2024-09
Fix: Supply the date the note was given and state whether the speaker is a current client, or remove the quote.

Confirm 1
Where: "shared with permission"
Depends on: Did the adviser provide any cash or non-cash compensation for the quote?
Cite: (b)(1)(i)(B)
Would apply: TE-05, TE-08

Confirm 2
Where: "a family I've worked with since 2022"
Depends on: Does the family have any material conflict of interest arising from its relationship with the adviser?
Cite: (b)(1)(iii)
Would apply: TE-06
